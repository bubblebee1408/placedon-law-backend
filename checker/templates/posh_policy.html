4. Display of this policy

The employer shall display the penal consequences of sexual harassment, and the order constituting the Internal Committee, at a conspicuous place at the workplace. [s.19(b)] This policy is displayed in fulfilment of that duty.

5. Consequences of non-compliance

Where an employer fails to constitute an Internal Committee, or to act as the Act requires, the employer is punishable by a fine which may extend to fifty thousand rupees. [s.26]
