# Personas

## Primary — the customer we build for
| Persona | Context | What they need from Placedon |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporate-law associate** | Law firm, 1-6 yrs PQE, does the first-pass work | Find provisions fast, with citations a partner will accept |
| **Corporate advocate** | Independent or small firm, drafts corporate documents | Confidence that a provision was current on the transaction date |
| **In-house legal counsel** | Company legal team, both user and buyer | Review documents without escalating everything to outside counsel |
| **Legal researcher** | Firm knowledge/research function | Traceable sources with effective dates |

## Secondary — users, contributors, not the buyer
| Persona | Role |
|---|---|
| Company Secretary | Domain expert; may use it; **not the primary customer** |
| CS trainee / paralegal | Workflow user inside a firm deal |
| Compliance professional | Adjacent user |

## Buyer vs user
| | |
|---|---|
| Uses it | Associate, advocate, in-house counsel, researcher |
| Approves the spend | Law-firm partner, or General Counsel in-house |

The buyer and user diverge in firms and converge for the independent advocate. Pricing and channel
must be designed for both shapes.

## Validation testers — accessible now, clearly labelled
Corporate-law students · junior associates · legal interns · in-house trainees · law-firm
researchers · advocates drafting corporate documents.

**Their feedback is early validation, never proof of lawyer demand.** Do not present law-student
feedback as professional validation. See `docs/EVIDENCE_PROTOCOL.md`.
