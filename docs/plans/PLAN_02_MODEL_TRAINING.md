# Model training — what to train, and what this project already declined

## Start with the two facts that constrain everything

**1. The hardware.** Measured, not estimated: Apple M1, **8 GB unified memory,
Metal only, no CUDA.** `qwen3.5` alone occupies 6.6 GB of it. The 8B QLoRA
fine-tune was cancelled on that basis, and the record says why in the right
words: *"This is arithmetic, not a judgement to revisit."*

**2. What was already measured and declined**, on this machine, against a frozen
70-case eval:

| Configuration | p@1 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| BM25 (shipped) | 0.71 | baseline |
| Dense (MiniLM-L6) | 0.73 | noise at n=70 |
| **RRF fusion** | **0.80** | **ADOPTED** |
| Learned reranker (7-fold held-out) | 0.83 ± 0.10 | **declined** — the ± swallows the gain |
| `gemma3:1b` + hybrid + schema | 0.45 | declined |
| `gemma3:1b` alone | 0.00 | declined |

A Harvey-Tenet-style post-training programme is not available to this project and
proposing one would contradict measurements already on the record.

## Why Harvey's data strategy cannot be copied

Harvey's answer to the legal "data desert" is to employ **hundreds of qualified
lawyers to write** golden examples — synthetic expert data for workflows that do
not exist on the open internet. That is a capital strategy, not a technical one.

This project has **zero lawyers** and one instrument awaiting attestation. Copying
the strategy is not a smaller version of the same plan; it is a different plan.

## What we have that Harvey does not

The label set for the one model worth training **already exists inside the corpus,
and it is the exact shape the hard problem needs.**

| Label type | Source | Why it is valuable |
|---|---|---|
| Positive pairs | (section text, its own amendment-footnote claim) — 434 parsed records, 24 corroborated | Real "text supports claim" examples |
| **Hard negatives** | **prior wording vs current wording of an amended span** | **Near-identical, legally opposite — precisely the "serves repealed law as current" failure** |
| Date negatives | same claim, wrong as-of date | Point-in-time discrimination |
| Instrument negatives | `ACT:…:S56` vs `RULE:…:R56` | Act-vs-Rule confusion |

The hard negatives are the asset. A generic NLI model trained on MNLI has never
seen two texts that differ by one clause and reverse a legal outcome. Ours are
generated by the amendment history itself, at no labelling cost.

**Unmeasured:** whether 434 records yield enough usable pairs after filtering.
That is the first experiment, and it costs nothing but an afternoon.

**And the approach is novel — verified.** No published paper builds a
hard-negative training set from pre- and post-amendment statutory wording. The
technique of mining hard negatives from NLI contradiction pairs is established
(supervised SimCSE), and structure-aware negatives from adjacent statutory
hierarchy are known to beat random ones in legal retrieval — but nobody has used
amendment history as the negative source. That is a genuine gap, not a crowded
field.

**The precedent that says the pipeline works:** at COLIEE Task 4 — statute-law
entailment, the closest published analogue to what we need — a team fine-tuning
DeBERTa pretrained on SNLI + MultiNLI placed 3rd of 8. That is exactly the
commercially-clean stack above, transferring to statutory entailment.

## The one model worth training

**A legal NLI head: does this claim follow from this span?**

Everything else in the stack is deterministic and works. The gap the system cannot
close without a model is entailment — checking that a produced sentence *follows
from* the served text. Inapplicable or misgrounded authority is a large share of
legal hallucination, and it is the failure a citation check cannot catch.

### Licences — verified, and one of them is a trap

| Resource | Licence | Usable? |
|---|---|---|
| **ContractNLI** (official Stanford source) | **CC BY 4.0** | **Yes** |
| ContractNLI (`kiddothe2b` HF mirror) | tagged **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0** | **No — same data, different licence.** Pull from the official source and check the LICENSE in the downloaded zip |
| SNLI | CC BY-SA 4.0 | Yes, with share-alike |
| CaseHOLD | Apache-2.0 per repo; the Harvard CAP commercial restriction lifted March 2024 | Likely — re-check the dataset card |
| BillSum | CC0-1.0 | Yes (but summarisation, not NLI) |
| MultiNLI | mixed per-genre | Verify per genre first |
| **ANLI** | **CC-BY-NC-4.0** | **No** |
| **ILDC, HLDC, IL-TUR, Pile of Law, MILPaC** | **CC-BY-NC / NC-SA** | **No.** IL-TUR also has no entailment task at all |
| AILA, NyayaAnumana | **UNVERIFIED** | Do not assume rights |
| **InLegalBERT** | **MIT** — verified on the model card | **Yes.** 5.4M Indian SC/HC documents. The base model |
| Legal-BERT (nlpaueb) | CC-BY-SA-4.0 | Copyleft — derivative *weights* inherit share-alike. Fallback only |

**The finding that decides the plan: no commercially-licensed Indian statutory
entailment dataset exists.** Not one. Every India-specific resource is
non-commercial, and the one clean Indian dataset (IndicLegalQA, CC BY 4.0) is
Q&A, not entailment.

So the Indian entailment data must be built in-house — which is exactly what the
corpus-mined pairs above are. That is not a workaround; it is the only legal path,
and we happen to already hold the raw material.

Constraints on the head:

- **Commercially clean data only**, per the table above.
- **Base:** InLegalBERT — MIT verified, India-pretrained, encoder-scale so LoRA
  fine-tuning is hours on one GPU, not a cluster.
- **Test a general encoder too.** The ContractNLI authors found in-domain
  pretraining did *not* help document-level NLI in their setup. Do not assume
  domain pretraining wins — measure it.
- **Compute:** a short-lived rented GPU, hours not weeks. Not this machine.
- **It runs inside the existing cascade** as E3–E6: E6 gates and may refuse but
  never accept; specialists bind role and quantity; E3 is the weak fallback.
  `NO_ANSWER` (abstention) stays distinct from `NOT_SUPPORTED` (refusal).

## How we will know it works — before it ships

The evaluation is preregistered, or it is a scoreboard.

1. **Write the question set and the metric before running anything.** The previous
   loop declined to tune a ranker against an eval authored in the same session,
   for exactly this reason: it would "launder the eval into a scoreboard."
2. **Two axes, not one:** `hallucinated = incorrect OR misgrounded`. A right answer
   from a wrong provision is a failure.
3. **No LLM-as-judge.** It inherits the recency bias under test. Deterministic
   scoring nuggets only.
4. **The metric that decides adoption:** false-accept rate on legal rows. A model
   that improves fluency and worsens false-accepts is rejected.
5. **Held-out, and the ± reported.** The learned reranker scored 0.83 ± 0.10 and
   was declined because the interval swallowed the gain. Same bar applies here.
6. **Mutation-test the eval itself.** Feed it a knowingly-wrong model. If the score
   does not fall, the eval is decoration.

## The staged plan

| Stage | Work | Gate before proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| **M0** | Mine the label set from the corpus; count usable pairs after filtering | Are there enough hard negatives to train on? If not, stop here |
| **M1** | Preregister the eval; measure a frontier model zero-shot as the baseline | Is the baseline already good enough? If yes, do not train |
| **M2** | PEFT fine-tune the NLI head on rented GPU | ≥8-point held-out lift, interval excluded from zero |
| **M3** | Ship into the E3–E6 cascade behind the existing gates | Full suite green; false-accept rate not worse |
| **M4** | Wire narration (Claude) in **shadow mode**, measured against reviewer decisions before any user sees it | Reviewer agreement measured, not assumed |

**M0 and M1 may well end the programme, and that is a success.** If a zero-shot
frontier model already clears the bar behind our gates, training is cost without
benefit — and the honest thing is to record that and move on, as this project did
with the reranker and the local model.

## What is explicitly not being built

- No foundation model. No "Indian legal reasoning" model. No data rights, no
  budget, and not the moat.
- **No live training on user data.** Fresh law flows through *retrieval updates* —
  a current, dated source database — never weight updates. Fine-tuning does not
  preserve exact amounts, dates or citations; new law contradicts old; and a
  correction or licence withdrawal must be traceably removable. A weight is not.
- No vector DB, graph DB, k8s or GPU fleet until a measurement demands one.
