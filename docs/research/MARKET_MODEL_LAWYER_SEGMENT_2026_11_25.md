## Buyer: In-House Legal Teams at Indian Companies

The old CS-based model is secondary. The primary buyer is the **corporate in-house legal department** — General Counsel, compliance heads, company secretaries embedded inside corporates — not independent CS practitioners.

## TAM — Total Addressable Market

**Indian corporate legal expenditure (FY26): ₹97,664 crore (~$10.35B)** across 4,546 listed companies reporting legal spend. [Source: Business Standard / ET data via TradingView]

The compliance + regulatory subset is the relevant slice. Industry commentary attributes the 13-17% annual growth primarily to compliance costs (DPDP Act, SEBI tightening, Companies Act amendments). **Inferred**: compliance/audit tooling is ~5-10% of legal spend → **TAM ≈ $500M-$1B** for compliance tools serving Indian corporates.

Cross-check: Ken Research sizes India's AI-powered legaltech & compliance market at **$423M (2025)**, growing to ~$1.96B by 2032 at 24.5% CAGR. This is the narrowest and most explicitly defined published estimate.

**Working TAM: ~$500M (2026)**

## SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market

PlacedOn targets **corporate-law compliance audit** (Companies Act 2013, SEBI LODR, Secretarial Standards — not contracts, not litigation, not DPDP).

Companies that need this:
- **~5,500 listed companies** (NSE+BSE) — mandatory secretarial audit u/s 204, board compliance
- **~1,200 large unlisted companies** exceeding s.204 thresholds (₹50cr capital / ₹250cr turnover)
- **~25,000 private companies** with CS requirement (paid-up capital ≥₹5cr) [Source: MCA annual report, inferred from CS appointment mandates]

**~31,700 companies** are the serviceable universe. Average compliance-audit tooling budget is unknown but: Nifty 500 companies average ₹1.36cr/year on total legal spend (₹68,210cr ÷ 500); smaller companies far less.

**Inferred SAM**: If 10% of legal spend is audit-amenable and 50% of the universe is reachable by a SaaS tool → **SAM ≈ $30-60M/year**.

## SOM — Year-1 Target

Realistic Y1 for a pre-revenue startup:
- Target: **100-200 companies** (Nifty 500 and large unlisted)
- Price: **₹5-15L/year** ($6K-$18K) per company
- **SOM ≈ $0.6M-$3.6M ARR**
- Conservative midpoint: **~$1.5M ARR** (150 companies × ₹10L)

## Why Lawyer Segment > CS Segment

1. **Spend**: In-house legal departments control 10-100x the budget of independent CS firms
2. **Pain**: Compliance cost growing 13-17% YoY — pressure to automate
3. **Mandate**: Increasing regulatory scrutiny (SEBI, MCA, DPDP) makes audit tools non-discretionary
4. **CS is a channel, not the buyer**: The CS often sits inside the legal department; selling to legal captures CS workflow too

## Risks & Gaps

- No authoritative count of Indian companies with in-house legal counsel (CLOC India data not public)
- Published market sizes vary by 10x depending on scope definition
- Willingness-to-pay for audit-specific (vs general compliance) tools is unvalidated
- Price point (₹5-15L) is inferred from enterprise SaaS norms, not from buyer interviews
