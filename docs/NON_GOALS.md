# Non-Goals

Not in the first version. Each has a reason, so the decision can be revisited on evidence rather
than on enthusiasm.

| Not building | Why |
|---|---|
| Every Indian statute | Twelve sections must be measured before breadth is earned |
| Every corporate document | Four classes; the scanner already failed by applying minutes checks to notices |
| Automated legal advice | We identify **potentially applicable** provisions. Advice is a lawyer's act |
| "Legally compliant" certification | A legal conclusion. We do not make it |
| Automatic filing or MCA submission | Transfers liability to us and depends on a portal we do not control |
| General legal chatbot | Puts us against Lexlegis and every frontier model, and abandons the wedge |
| A document generator | ComplyRelax ships it free to 2029. See `PRODUCT_DIRECTION_V2.md` §2.4 |
| Unrestricted web scraping | Source policy. MCA WAF, RBI caching prohibition, robots restrictions |
| Training on Reddit or user-generated content | Community posts discover language and pain, never legal authority |
| Fine-tuning or a foundation model | No data rights, no budget, and not the moat |
| Vector search at current corpus size | ~470 sections. Sciavolino et al. EMNLP 2021: BM25 wins on entity-rich exact match |
| Team workspaces, marketplace, escrow | Not the wedge |
| Company Secretaries as the primary customer | Scope change 20 Aug. They remain domain contributors |
